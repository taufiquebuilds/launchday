# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-02)

First release.

- Eight-phase launch workflow in `SKILL.md`: understand, name check, audit, README, demo, release, launch kit, hand-off.
- `scripts/name_check.py`: checks a name on GitHub, crates.io, npm, PyPI and Homebrew.
- `scripts/audit.py`: launch-readiness score out of 100, with a fix list.
- `scripts/launch_time.py`: next Tuesday to Thursday slot at 8:30 AM US Eastern, shown in your time zone, following daylight-saving rules. Skips US election weeks, Thanksgiving week, Christmas to New Year and 4 July.
- `scripts/social_preview.sh`: 1280×640 social preview from a GIF, video or screenshot, padded instead of cropped.
- References for README, demo, release CI and launch posts.
